ZTV Han Regular / ZTV Han Medium

Derived from Adobe Source Han Sans CN 2.005, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The variable source is instantiated at weights 400 and 500, then subset to the
GB2312 repertoire, ASCII and common punctuation. Font family and PostScript
names are changed to ZTV Han to respect the upstream Reserved Font Names.
Characters outside this subset use Android's normal system font fallback.
Copyright and license name records are preserved. See source-han-license.txt.

IBM Plex Sans Regular is distributed unmodified; see plex-license.txt.

Rebuild: tools/prepare-tv-fonts.py, using the official upstream source font.
